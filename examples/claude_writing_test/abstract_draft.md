# Abstract

**Background:** Identifying prognostic biomarkers is essential for understanding patient survival outcomes in cancer. This study aimed to identify genes associated with prognosis through a systematic transcriptomic screening approach.

**Methods:** Differential expression analysis was performed using the edgeR package to identify genes with altered expression between tumor and normal samples. Subsequently, univariate Cox regression analysis was conducted to evaluate the prognostic significance of the differentially expressed genes using survival analysis methods.

**Results:** A total of 4,774 genes were identified as differentially expressed, with 2,797 genes upregulated and 1,977 genes downregulated in tumor samples. Univariate Cox regression analysis identified 16 genes with significant associations with patient prognosis, including 9 risk factors and 7 protective factors. Representative genes included B4GALNT2, BCAT1, CHST11, DHPS, and DIO1.

**Conclusion:** This systematic screening approach identified candidate prognostic genes that warrant further investigation as potential biomarkers for patient survival outcomes.
