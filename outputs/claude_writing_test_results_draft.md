# Results

## Differential Expression Analysis

To establish a candidate gene pool for subsequent prognostic screening, we performed differential expression analysis between tumor and normal samples. A total of 4,774 genes were identified as differentially expressed. Among these, 2,797 genes (58.6%) were upregulated and 1,977 genes (41.4%) were downregulated in tumor samples, indicating a predominant pattern of transcriptional activation in the tumor phenotype.

## Univariate Cox Regression Analysis

To evaluate the prognostic significance of the differentially expressed genes, we conducted univariate Cox regression analysis using patient survival data. Sixteen genes demonstrated significant associations with patient prognosis. Among these prognostic genes, 9 were identified as risk factors and 7 as protective factors. Representative genes identified through this analysis included B4GALNT2, BCAT1, CHST11, DHPS, and DIO1, which warrant further investigation as potential prognostic biomarkers.
